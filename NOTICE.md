## insightface models

Face ranking uses insightface's pretrained `buffalo_l` models, which insightface licenses for non-commercial research use only. This repo's licence does not cover them. Commercial use needs a licence from insightface (see https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface#license). `generate.py` works without them. They are downloaded on first use of `rank.py`; this repo does not redistribute them.
